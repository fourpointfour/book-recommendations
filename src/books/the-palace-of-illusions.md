## Why I recommend this book

This gave me such a fresh perspective of Mahabharata from Draupadi's perspective. The story fully explored the human side of things when we have seen every where else how Mahabharata is almost always considered a battle of gods and wits and egos - and even though I agree and like that representation, what everyone else misses is the portrayal of the daily lives after the war, in the camps, how the warriors would be coming back home, how the family would be reacting, how the women of the war felt. Something always felt missing about the story I have grown up hearing. This one fills that void for me.

And oh, how can I forget the portrayal of Krishna here, he is most definitely the most complex character I have ever read in any book or story. I need to explore Krishna more.
