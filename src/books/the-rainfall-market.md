## Why I recommend this book

This one was a very slow read for me, I took over 2 months and read this one in pieces. But I do remember one feeling I had everytime I read this book. The feeling was of comfort and very anti-suspense, what this means is that I probably was able to understand the flow and I also knew that whatever would come will be something that will make me feel soothing rather than the feeling of twists and turns that comes whenever a suspense is revealed.

### Favorite ideas

1. I liked how every orb was created using something taken from the human world and how those humane-things shaped the way things worked in the Dokkaebi world.
2. In my head, I have assumed that the way humans are made up of all the emotions, happy and sad, uplifting and depressing, good and bad, the rainbow orb itself represents the idea of everything combined.
