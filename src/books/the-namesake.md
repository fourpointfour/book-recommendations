## Why I recommend this book

At the time of writing this review, I do not know why do I recommend this book. All I know is that I was hooked the entire time I was reading this book and I do not know what else to think of it. The story is unsettling for sure. It was unsettling and uncomfortable to me at times - I wanted to continue reading to know what happens next and I wanted to stop because I was afraid to discover what happens next and I ended up looking for a term for what this feeling is called and apparently there's a name, **Reader's anxiety**.

There were so many instances when I just wanted to close it for the night and go to sleep, some instances I slept off peacefully and the other times I just kept second guessing myself whether I would have been better off finishing the book or continuing it further.
